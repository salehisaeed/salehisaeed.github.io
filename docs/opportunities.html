Opportunities

MSc and BSc thesis projects, PhD and postdoc opportunities in CFD, OpenFOAM and machine learning for fluid flows with Saeed Salehi at Linköping University.

I enjoy working with motivated students and researchers interested in computational fluid dynamics, high-fidelity simulation and data-driven methods. Below are the current openings and how to get in touch.

MSc and BSc thesis projects

I supervise thesis projects in areas such as:

  • development of CFD methods in OpenFOAM
  • high-fidelity simulation of turbulent and unsteady flows
  • data-driven and machine learning methods for flow analysis and control

If you study at Linköping University and would like to align your thesis with my research, feel free to contact me.

Postdocs and fellowships

There are no funded postdoc openings at the moment. If you have your own funding or plan to apply for a fellowship, I am happy to discuss possible projects. I will support strong applications for external scholarships and fellowships and can host you at Linköping University.

Examples of funding for PhD candidates

  • Swedish national graduate schools (e.g. WASP)
  • Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Networks
  • Other national or EU-funded PhD scholarship programmes

Examples of funding for postdocs

  • Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation postdoc grants
  • MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships
  • National mobility or international exchange programmes

How to get in touch

If you are interested in joining, email me with:

  1. a short CV
  2. a brief description of your research interests and how they connect to my research
  3. your background in CFD, programming and machine learning
  4. if relevant, your plan for applying for a scholarship or external funding
